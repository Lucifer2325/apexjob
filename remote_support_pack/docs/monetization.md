# Monetization Guide

How to turn Remote Support Pack into revenue streams as an IT support professional.

---

## Business Models

### Model 1: Per-Check Diagnostic Service

**Target**: Individuals, small businesses needing one-off diagnostics

**How It Works**:
- Customer pays for each diagnostic run
- You run RSP, collect report, deliver analysis
- Turn-around time: 24 hours
- Pricing: $25–$75 per check

**Positioning**:
- "Windows Performance Audit"
- "Malware & Bloatware Cleanup Consultation"
- "System Health Check"

**Example Service Package**:
```
Basic System Diagnostic - $35
├─ Run full diagnostic
├─ Identify 5 top issues
├─ Send report
├─ Email support for 7 days
└─ No remediation

Premium with Remediation - $75
├─ Everything in Basic
├─ Fix top 3 issues (cleanup, service optimization, etc.)
├─ Follow-up diagnostic in 1 week
└─ Phone support for 30 days
```

**Revenue Calculation**:
- 10 checks/month × $50 avg = $500/month
- 20 checks/month × $50 avg = $1,000/month (part-time)
- 40 checks/month × $50 avg = $2,000/month (full-time)

### Model 2: Monthly Monitoring Subscription

**Target**: Small businesses, remote workers wanting ongoing health checks

**How It Works**:
- Set up recurring monthly (or weekly) diagnostics
- Automate runs via scheduled task
- Collect reports centrally
- Alert client if critical issue detected
- Tiered pricing based on machine count

**Pricing Structure**:
```
Tier 1: 1–5 machines    $15/machine/month ($15–$75)
Tier 2: 6–15 machines   $12/machine/month ($72–$180)
Tier 3: 16+ machines    $10/machine/month ($160+)
```

**Example Customer**:
- Small law firm: 12 machines
- Cost: 12 × $12 = $144/month = **$1,728/year**
- Your effort: 30 min setup + 15 min/month review = ~5 hours total
- Revenue/hour: ~$300–400/hour over the year

**Revenue Calculation**:
- 5 customers × $100/month avg = $500/month
- 15 customers × $100/month avg = $1,500/month (sustainable)
- 30 customers × $100/month avg = $3,000/month (requires team)

### Model 3: Incident Troubleshooting with RSP

**Target**: Businesses experiencing system issues

**How It Works**:
- Customer calls with problem ("My computer is slow")
- Use RSP diagnostic to quickly identify root cause
- Charge hourly for diagnostic + remediation
- Bundle diagnostic into support package

**Pricing**:
```
1-hour incident support session - $75–$150
├─ Remote access to machine
├─ Run RSP diagnostic
├─ Troubleshoot & fix issue
├─ Provide report & recommendations
└─ Email follow-up
```

**Positioning**:
- Faster resolution (RSP vs. manual debugging)
- Data-driven troubleshooting
- Better client confidence

**Revenue Calculation**:
- 5 incident calls/month × $100 avg = $500/month
- 10 incident calls/month × $100 avg = $1,000/month
- Plus upsell to ongoing monitoring

### Model 4: MSP White-Label Service

**Target**: Managed Service Providers wanting RSP capabilities

**How It Works**:
- License RSP to MSPs for their use
- They deploy to their customers
- They charge their customers
- You get licensing fee or revenue share

**Pricing Example**:
- Licensing fee: $500–2,000/year per MSP
- Or: 15–20% revenue share on their customer monitoring fees

**Why MSPs Use This**:
- Existing MSPs avoid building their own tool
- Focus on selling, not developing
- RSP handles diagnostics; they handle customer relationships

**Revenue Potential**:
- 10 MSP partners × $1,000/year = $10,000/year (passive)
- 10 MSP partners × 20% revenue share (if they make $1,000/month) = $20,000/year+

### Model 5: Consulting & Implementation

**Target**: Enterprises wanting to deploy RSP at scale

**How It Works**:
- Custom RSP deployment plan
- Integration with their existing tools (syslog, SIEM, etc.)
- Staff training
- Ongoing support

**Services Offered**:
```
RSP Implementation Project - $2,000–$10,000
├─ Discovery & scoping (8 hours)
├─ Customization & configuration (16 hours)
├─ Deployment planning (8 hours)
├─ Staff training (4 hours)
├─ 30 days post-launch support
└─ Documentation & runbooks

Ongoing Support Plan - $500–$2,000/month
├─ Monthly optimization calls
├─ Report analysis & recommendations
├─ Tool updates & patches
├─ Technical support (2-hour response time)
└─ Annual roadmap planning
```

**Revenue Potential**:
- 2 implementation projects/quarter × $5,000 = $10,000/quarter = $40,000/year
- Plus 2 ongoing support contracts × $1,000/month = $24,000/year
- Total: $64,000+/year

### Model 7: Managed SOC / Monitoring Center

**Target**: Enterprise customers needing managed services

**How It Works**:
- Set up centralized monitoring dashboard
- Deploy RSP to 100+ customer machines
- 24/7 monitoring center reviews reports
- Alert escalation & incident response

**Pricing**:
- $25–$50 per machine per month
- Minimum contract: 25 machines = $625–$1,250/month

**Revenue Potential**:
- 10 customers × 50 machines × $30/month = $15,000/month = $180,000/year

**Requirements**:
- SOC staff (3–5 people initially)
- Dashboard/SIEM platform
- Incident response playbooks

- [ ] Download RSP and run on 3–5 test machines

## Pricing Psychology

**Factors Affecting Price**:
| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Your experience | ⬆️ Higher if certified (CompTIA, MCSA, etc.) |
| Local market | ⬆️ Higher in SF/NYC, lower in rural areas |
| Customer size | ⬆️ Enterprise pays 10x more than small business |
| Urgency | ⬆️ Emergency support (24/7) = premium pricing |
| Complexity | ⬆️ Complex systems = higher fees |
| Competition | ⬇️ More competitors = lower pricing |

**Pricing Formula**:
```
(Hourly Rate × Hours) + (Complexity Multiplier) + (Urgency Surcharge)

Example:
- Base hourly: $75
- Diagnostic time: 1 hour = $75
- Complexity: 1.5x (complex system) = $112.50
- Urgency: +$25 (after-hours) = $137.50 → Round to $150
```

## Profitability Example

**Scenario**: Freelancer offering monthly monitoring

**Assumptions**:
- 10 customers × 5 machines each = 50 machines
- Price: $12/machine/month
- Monthly revenue: 50 × $12 = $600/month = $7,200/year
- Time: 2 hours setup + 30 min/month review = 8 hours total/year
- Hourly rate: $7,200 ÷ 8 = **$900/hour** (very profitable!)

**vs. Traditional Hourly Support**:
- 10 customers × 5 hours/month = 50 hours/month
- Hourly rate: $75/hour
- Monthly revenue: 50 × $75 = $3,750
- But much more time spent

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake 1**: Pricing too low  
→ You'll be busy but poor. Charge more than you think.

❌ **Mistake 2**: Over-promising  
→ Set clear expectations upfront (e.g., "2-day turnaround")

❌ **Mistake 3**: Ignoring security & compliance  
→ Get liability insurance, sign NDAs, follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md)

❌ **Mistake 4**: No written contracts  
→ Use simple SOW (Statement of Work) template

❌ **Mistake 5**: Bad customer selection  
→ Choose customers who value your service, not just lowest price
