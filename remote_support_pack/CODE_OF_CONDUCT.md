# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive community for all members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow
- Giving credit to others for their contributions

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing)
- Sexual language and imagery, and sexual harassment
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing:
**conduct@example.com**

Include:
- Description of the incident
- Participants involved (usernames)
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant links or screenshots

All reports are confidential and will be reviewed promptly.

### Consequences

Community leaders will determine appropriate responses, which may include:

1. **Verbal Warning**: First-time, minor infractions
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Repeated violations or serious behavior
3. **Permanent Ban**: Severe harassment, threats, or illegal activity

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project communication channels (Discord, email, etc.)
- Community events and meetups
- Any official project space

---

## Inclusive Language

Please use inclusive language:
- ✅ Use neutral pronouns (they/them) or ask for preferred pronouns
- ✅ Avoid gendered terms (use "folks" instead of "guys")
- ✅ Acknowledge different backgrounds and experiences
- ❌ Avoid slurs, stereotypes, or offensive language

---

## How We Help

If you're unsure about behavior or feel uncomfortable:
- **Ask questions**: No question is too basic
- **Request guidance**: We're happy to explain community norms
- **Suggest improvements**: We value constructive feedback
